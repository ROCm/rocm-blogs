# Yamini Preethi Kamisetty

Yamini Preethi Kamisetty is a Machine Learning Engineer at AMD, where she specializes in optimizing performance of Large Language Models on AMD Instinct™ GPUs. As part of the MLPerf team, she focuses on LLM workload optimization, MLPerf benchmarking, and customer workloads. Yamini works on advanced LLM performance optimization, MLPerf benchmarking, and agentic RAG systems, pushing the boundaries of what’s possible for high‑performance AI at scale.
