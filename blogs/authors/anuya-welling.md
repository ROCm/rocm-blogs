# Anuya Welling

Anuya Welling is a Senior Member of Technical Staff at AMD in the AIOSS Ecosystem team. She works to enable and optimize machine learning and AI libraries on the AMD ROCm™ platform, helping developers run high-performance AI workloads efficiently on AMD GPUs. She has previous experience in High Performance Computing and Parallel Programming, and enjoys working on 0-to-1 projects that build scalable, production-ready systems.
