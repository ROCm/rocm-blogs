# Elliott Delaye

Elliott Delaye is a Senior Fellow and technical architect of AI software across GPU and NPU products at AMD.  He is focused on the RyzenAI APUs developing compilers and frameworks for running all varieties of AI models, specializing on large language models.  His work is published in various conferences and journals and has authored over 35 issued US patents.  He received his Masters degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Carnegie Mellon University.
