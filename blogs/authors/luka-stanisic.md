Luka Stanisic is a Senior Member of Technical Staff (SMTS) Software System Design Engineer in the Data Center
GPU Software Solutions group. He obtained his PhD in Computer Science from the University of Grenoble (France)
where he specialized in simulations of task-based applications on heterogeneous hardware. He has over a decade
of experience at research institutes, supercomputing centers and industry in HPC application optimizations
using MPI + OpenMP + HIP/CUDA. His research interests are focused on performance optimization, benchmarking,
analysis, modeling, simulation and visualization.
