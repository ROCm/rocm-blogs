# Bowen Bao

Bowen Bao is a Principal Member of the Technical Staff (PMTS) Engineer in AMD’s AIG-AIE Quark team, where he focuses on model quantization and inference framework optimization for AMD AI accelerators. Prior to joining AMD, he worked as software engineer tech lead at Microsoft AI frameworks team, leading the development of PyTorch - ONNX model deployment.
