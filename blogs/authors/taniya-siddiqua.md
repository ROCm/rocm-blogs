# Taniya Siddiqua

Taniya Siddiqua is a Director of Silicon Design Engineering at AMD, working on system-on-chip and fabric design. Taniya focuses on the scale-up interconnect and resiliency architecture that allows large numbers of AMD Instinct GPUs to operate as a single, dependable system.
