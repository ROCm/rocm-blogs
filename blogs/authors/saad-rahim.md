# Saad Rahim

Saad is a software product manager for the Instinct GPU software stack responsible
for packaging, documentation, drivers, and runtimes. He is the driving force behind
launching [ROCm blogs](https://rocm.blogs.amd.com) and is on its steering committee. His product management role
allows him to be involved in every facet of GPGPU development at AMD.

In a previous role in engineering,
Saad worked on the transition to the AMD clang compiler from hcc, built the CI system
for the ROCm math & communication libraries and lead the transition to
docs-as-code for ROCm documentation. He was also the architect for the first release
of ROCm on Windows. He probably contributed to every single repository that is part of ROCm.

Saad is fond of skiing, biking, and enjoying the Canadian Rockies with his family. He also
loves driving ROCM, his red [Audi S3](https://github.com/saadrahim/).

Since 2007, Saad's jobs kept him in the GPUGPU industry. He has only worked for A-list companies: Aga AB, Advanced NPD,
[Acceleware](https://www.acceleware.com) and AMD. He holds an MSc and BSc in Electrical engineering
from the University of Calgary.
