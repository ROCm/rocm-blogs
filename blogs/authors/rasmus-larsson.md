# Rasmus Larsson

Rasmus is a Senior Member of the Technical Staff focused on AI for enterprises. In previous roles at AMD and Silo AI, he built and deployed machine learning models, led data science projects from prototype to production and helped organizations upskill staff and create practical internal AI tools and processes.

Rasmus holds an MSc in Industrial Engineering and Management from the Royal Institute of Technology and has worked as a consultant across multiple industries.
