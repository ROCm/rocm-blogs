# Maanasa Mohanambal Sathianarayanan

Maanasa Mohanambal Sathianarayanan is a Technical Marketing professional at AMD with over 8 years of
experience in the semiconductor industry across FPGA, CPU, and GPU products. She is passionate about
AI infrastructure, data center technologies, and high-performance computing. She enjoys translating
complex engineering innovations into clear, engaging technical content that connects cutting-edge
technology with real-world impact. She works closely with engineering teams to communicate the value
of AMD AI and data center solutions. Outside of work, Maanasa enjoys ice cream, Indian food, and
watching beach sunsets.
