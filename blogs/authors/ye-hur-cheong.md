# Ye Hur Cheong

Ye Hur Cheong is an AI researcher and DevOps engineer with 10+ years of hands-on experience, spanning deep learning, generative AI, and physical sciences. Before working in LLM related researches, he worked in weather forecasting and simulation, including applying deep learning to satellite imagery for radar nowcasting, provides a strong foundation in data-intensive systems. He currently focuses on Large Language Models, with specialization in model architecture, quantization, and deployment. His work involves building end-to-end LLM deployment: optimizing inference for low latency, and scalable production systems.
