# Layla Frischman

Layla Frischman is a product manager on AMD's AI SW Product Management team, with prior experience in program management and QA at AMD. She holds a BASc in Industrial Engineering and a minor in Artificial Intelligence Engineering from the University of Toronto, and is a certified product manager. Layla focuses on making technical AI concepts accessible to diverse audiences, whether working with engineering teams, stakeholders, or customers.
