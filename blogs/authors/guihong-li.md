Guihong Li is an MTS Software Engineer at AMD, where he focuses on efficient LLM training/inference algorithm-system co-design and exploring the hybrid LLM architectures. He earned his Ph.D. from the University of Texas at Austin in 2024; during his PhD, his research focused on the zero-shot Neural Architecture Search (NAS), fine-tuning and inference efficiency for deep networks, and trustworthy machine learning methods for generative models.
