Mengmeng Ge is an AI Engineer at AMD’s AIG team. His current work focuses on video generation models, including improving video quality through post-training and accelerating video generation through pruning and distillation. He received his Bachelor’s degree from BUPT in 2021 and has been working at AMD since 2025. In addition, he has research experience in customized image and video generation.
