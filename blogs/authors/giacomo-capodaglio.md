# Giacomo Capodaglio

Giacomo Capodaglio is an SMTS Software Applications Eng. in the HPC and SovAI Customer Enablement
team working on assisting partners and customers with installing software, programming and optimizing
on AMD GPUs. He has a PhD in Applied Mathematics from Texas Tech University and a Master’s in
Energy Engineering from the University of Bologna (Italy). Prior to joining AMD, Giacomo was a
Scientist at Los Alamos National Laboratory, working on numerical methods for the ocean and sea
ice components of DOE’s climate model E3SM. Besides climate, Giacomo’s work includes urban
flooding modeling, uncertainty quantification, probability density estimation, and numerical
methods development for nonlocal problems.
