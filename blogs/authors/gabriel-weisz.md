# Gabriel Weisz

Gabe is a Fellow in the AI Group at AMD, where he gets the best neural network models to train and inference really fast on AMD's best GPUs.
In the past he's worked on software and hardware at Microsoft, USC's Information Sciences Institute, and at a number of startup companies.
He holds PhD and MS degrees in Computer Science from Carnegie Mellon University, and a BS degree with a double major in Computer Science and Electrical Engineering from Cornell University.
