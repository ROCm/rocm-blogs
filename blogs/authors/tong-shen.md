# Tong Shen

Tong Shen is a Senior Member of Technical Staff in the AI Group at AMD. His work focuses on image generation, including efficient model design, training and model distillation. Prior to joining AMD, he was a Research Engineer at JD.com, where he worked on a variety of computer vision tasks such as segmentation, image translation and generation. He received his Master’s degree in Robotics from KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Sweden, and his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Adelaide, Australia.
