# Georgy Krivoruchko

Georgy Krivoruchko is currently a Member of Technical Staff at AMD, where he focuses on the ROCm software stack and customer enablement across the MEA region.

With over 25 years of experience in software development, he has worked across a wide range of domains. Prior to joining AMD, he contributed to key technologies at Intel, including the OpenVINO toolkit, media frameworks such as OneVPL/MediaSDK and vendor's MFT libraries, as well as GPU drivers. He also brings extensive expertise in network and web development, database systems, enterprise-level systems, and automation.
