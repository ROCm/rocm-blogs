# Raghav Goyal

Raghav Goyal is a Member of Technical Staff on the GenAI team at AMD, where he works on Physical AI, including Vision-Language-Action (VLA) and World-Action Models (WAMs) for robot manipulation. Before AMD, he was at Samsung AI Center Toronto. He holds a PhD in computer vision from the University of British Columbia, focused on data-efficient learning for image and video understanding, and interned at Google Research and Meta AI. Earlier, at Twenty Billion Neurons, he co-created the Something-Something video dataset. He holds an Integrated M.Tech. from IIT Delhi.
