# Juuso Korhonen

Juuso Korhonen is a Member of Technical Staff (MTS) at AMD Silo AI's kernel team. His expertise includes GPU kernel development and performance optimization for generative AI workloads. Recently he co-authored a paper “Fused-Softmax for efficient quantized attention” about accelerating the attention kernel by fusing the softmax operations to matrix core instructions on CDNA 4. Prior to joining AMD Silo AI, Juuso worked on deep learning based 3D modeling and rendering. Juuso holds a Master of Science in Computer Science from Aalto University.
