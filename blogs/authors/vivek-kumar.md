# Vivek Kumar

Vivek is a Senior Member of Technical Staff (SMTS) at AMD, working on the open‑source ROCm software stack to accelerate AI and ML workloads on AMD Instinct GPUs. Before joining AMD, he worked at Intel, contributing to DSP kernels, graph compiler, and the PyTorch AI framework for hardware accelerators.
