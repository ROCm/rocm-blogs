# Vladimir Indic

Vladimir Inđić is a SMTS Software Development Engineer at AMD's ROCm Profiler team, focused on intra-kernel profiling with an emphasis on Program Counter (PC) Sampling for GPU performance analysis. Prior to joining AMD, he worked on performance analysis of GPU-accelerated applications and contributed to open-source development on profiling tools for AMD GPUs. He also spent several years researching asynchronous sampling techniques and tooling support for parallel programming runtimes, with a focus on improving performance measurement for high-performance computing applications, and has five years of experience as a teaching assistant in computer science and software engineering. Vladimir holds an MSc in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Novi Sad, Faculty of Technical Sciences, where his thesis focused on tools for measuring the performance of parallel programs.
