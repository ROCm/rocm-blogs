# Wei Huang

Wei Huang is a Senior Member of Technical Staff (SMTS) in the Training at Scale team. His expertise includes performance optimization and troubleshooting for large language model (LLM) training workloads. He is also experienced in the design and development of high-performance computing (HPC) kernels and collective communication primitives.
