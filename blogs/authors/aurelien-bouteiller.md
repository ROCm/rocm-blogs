# Aurelien Bouteiller

Dr. Aurelien Bouteiller is a Principal Member of Technical Staff (PMTS) software engineer at AMD, focusing on improving performance and scope of GPU support in HPC and AI communication libraries. He is a core developer of the RCCL GIN, and rocSHMEM GPU initiated communication libraries and a long-time contributor to Open MPI. He holds leading organization roles in the MPI Forum, OpenSHMEM, and PMIx standardization bodies. He received a PhD from the University of Paris.
