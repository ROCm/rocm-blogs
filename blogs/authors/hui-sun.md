# Hui Sun

Hui Sun is a MTS Software Development Engineer in AMD's AIG-AIE Solutions team. He focuses on enabling and optimizing generative AI models, including Stable Diffusion, for AMD NPUs. He received his Ph.D. in Geophysics from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2019, where his research focused on deep learning.
