Mohammad Norouzi is a Senior Member of Technical Staff (SMTS) and Software Development Engineer in AMD’s AI Open-Source Software (AIOSS) group.

He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Technical University of Darmstadt, Germany, where he gained extensive experience in developing and optimizing compilers and parallelization tools.

Since joining AMD in 2022, he has been contributing to the development and optimization of the ROCm Data Science (ROCm-DS) ecosystem, including hipDF and its underlying libraries such as libhipcxx.
