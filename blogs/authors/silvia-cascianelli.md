# Silvia Cascianelli

Dr. Silvia Cascianelli is an Assistant Professor with the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia, and a member of the European Laboratory for Learning and Intelligent Systems. She is active in the field of Multimodal AI, generative models, and document generation and understanding.
