# Istvan Kiss

Istvan Kiss is an MTS Software Development Engineer in the AI Group at AMD, specializing in high-performance computing and GPU acceleration. With 14+ years of experience, he focuses on parallel algorithms, performance analysis, GPU kernel optimization, and accelerating CT reconstruction and SPECT Monte Carlo pipelines across CUDA, HIP, and OpenCL. He has contributed to rocPRIM, rocThrust, hipCUB, rocRAND, and the OpenCL SDK, and co-authored the ROCm handbooks, including the AMD ROCm Programming Guide and AMD ROCm Optimization Guide.
