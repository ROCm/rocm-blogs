# Kenneth J. Roche

Kenneth Roche is a Senior Software Development Manager at AMD focusing on vLLM and distributed inference solutions, and affiliate faculty at the University of Washington in the Applied Mathematics and Physics departments. In 2025, he was a co-recipient of the US Department of Energy Secretary’s Honor Award as a member of the Exascale Computing Project (ECP) Leadership Team that led the design and deployment of the first exascale supercomputer. He lives in Seattle, Washington.
