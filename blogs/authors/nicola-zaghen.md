# Nicola Zaghen

Nicola Zaghen is a Senior Member of Technical Staff at AMD specializing in GPU compilers and kernel performance optimization for AI workloads. His work focuses on Triton support for AMD Instinct GPUs, including compiler lowering and LDS optimizations for high-performance matrix kernels.
