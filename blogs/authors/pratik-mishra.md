# Pratik Mishra

Pratik Mishra is part of AMD Research and Advanced Development, focusing on AI infrastructure at scale, fault-tolerance, and GPU–storage optimizations. Previously at Samsung Semiconductors, he was responsible for R&D efforts to adapt emerging high-performance intelligent NVMe storage technologies into data-centric environments. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from Iowa State University specializing in large-scale distributed storage systems, and has authored multiple publications and patents in computing systems, memory and storage technologies.
