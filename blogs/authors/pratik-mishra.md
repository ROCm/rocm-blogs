# Pratik Mishra

Pratik Mishra is part of AMD Research and Advanced Development, driving pathfinding efforts to address infrastructure challenges for deploying AI at scale. His work includes building robust full-stack solutions, enabling fault-tolerance, and optimizing data-movement between dense GPU clusters and distributed storage systems. Previously, he worked at Samsung Semiconductors responsible for R&D efforts to adapt emerging high-performance intelligent NVMe storage technologies into complex data-centric environments. Pratik holds a Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from Iowa State University specializing in large-scale distributed storage systems, and has authored multiple publications and patents in computing systems, storage, and memory subsystems.
