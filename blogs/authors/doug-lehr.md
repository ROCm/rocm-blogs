Doug Lehr is a Principal Member of the Technical Staff (PMTS) in AMD's AGS group, where he specializes in framework level optimizations for LLM inference workloads.  His areas of expertise are around performance analysis and debugging, as well as framework level code, including vllm, pytorch and others.
