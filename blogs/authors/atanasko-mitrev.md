# Atanasko Mitrev

Atanasko Boris Mitrev holds an MSc in Intelligent Systems, specializing in robotics and AI for autonomous vehicles. He brings extensive software development experience and applies machine learning techniques to a variety of client projects. As co-chair of the Autoware Foundation ADAS Working Group, he focuses on the development of the VisionPilot L2/L2+ system.
