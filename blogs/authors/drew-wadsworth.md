# Drew Wadsworth

Drew Wadsworth is a Senior Systems Research Engineer at Together AI, where he focuses on inference optimization and deployment. He received a BS and MS in Computer Science from Stanford University. His technical and research interests include parallel programming, compilers, DSLs for AI accelerators, and modern AI inference systems.
