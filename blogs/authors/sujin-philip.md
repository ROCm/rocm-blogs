# Sujin Philip

Sujin Philip is a Senior Member of Technical Staff at AMD, where he contributes to the AI Open-Source Software Solutions team. His work focuses on developing and optimizing open-source AI libraries within the ROCm-DS ecosystem. Sujin has extensive experience in implementing and tuning highly parallel GPU algorithms, primarily in the scientific visualization domain. He holds a master’s degree in computing from the University of Utah, specializing in Graphics and Visualization.
