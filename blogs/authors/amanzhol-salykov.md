# Amanzhol Salykov

Amanzhol is a Senior Member of Technical Staff at AMD, specializing in ML inference optimization and GPU kernel development. His recent work focuses on prototyping advanced AI/ML systems and optimizing their performance both at the model level and through efficient hardware implementation. He has a strong background in machine learning, mathematical modeling, and control, and holds a master's degree in Automation and Robotic Systems from the Vienna University of Technology.
