# Fuwei Yang

Fuwei Yang is a Manager Software Development Engineer in the AI Group of AMD. His team focus on training optimization of AI models in AMD Instinct GPUs. Prior to joining AMD, he worked as algorithm engineer in Megvii. He received his bachelor’s degree from Zhejiang University and master’s degree from Tsinghua University.
