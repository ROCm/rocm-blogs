# Daniel Gustafsson

Daniel Gustafsson is a Senior Member of Technical Staff - Program Manager, with a focus on Enterprise AI. At AMD, AMD Silo AI and in previous roles he's contributed to building and designing AI solutions and led AI projects and programs for organizations across various industries. 

Daniel holds a MSc in Information Systems, a BSc in Design of Information Systems as well as a BSc in Business and Economics.
