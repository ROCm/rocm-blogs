# Julia Zhang

Julia Zhang is a Senior Software Engineer on the Kernels team at Together AI, where she works on GPU kernel development and inference optimization. Her recent work focuses on high-performance attention kernels and low-level optimizations for AI inference. Her technical interests include parallel programming, GPU systems, and performance engineering. Previously, she worked on low-level systems and performance engineering, optimizing networking and video infrastructure for latency, throughput, and computational efficiency.
