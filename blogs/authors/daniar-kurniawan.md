# Daniar Kurniawan

Daniar Kurniawan leads the AI Software group at MangoBoost, which optimizes LLM inference systems from engine optimization in vLLM and SGLang down to the kernel level. He also leads MangoBoost’s MLPerf Inference efforts, including several world-first submissions such as the first heterogeneous-GPU setup and the first multi-region cluster, and record-setting results on AMD Instinct GPUs. Daniar holds a PhD in Computer Science, and his work sits at the intersection of distributed systems and AI, with a focus on making large-model inference faster and cheaper on open hardware.
