# Jeremy Arnold

Jeremy is a Fellow at AMD, where he leads data center system performance analysis of GPU-accelerated machine learning and HPC applications. His work centers on understanding and improving how AMD Instinct GPUs perform on real workloads, from end-to-end serving down to the underlying kernels and fabric. Earlier in his career he spent two decades in software and system performance engineering at IBM, and he remains a long-time contributor to the SPEC power committee.
