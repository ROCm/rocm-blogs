# Liam Berry

Liam is a Product Management intern at AMD and a student at McMaster University, studying Mechatronics and Biomedical Engineering.

Liam has worked on technical documentation for the Instinct data center GPUs, ROCm releases, and the amd.com website, authored and contributed to maintaining the ROCm Blogs site, written software requirements for release branching, documentation, docker repositories, Windows and Linux packaging, researching and building a testing suite to benchmark kernel launch latency, as well as numerous other endeavors.

When he's not at work, Liam enjoys rock climbing, drawing, and skiing alongside learning new skills and hobbies.
