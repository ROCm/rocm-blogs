# Travis Schluessler

Travis Schluessler is a Fellow and GPU Performance Architect at AMD, where he leads engineering teams to deliver peak performance on data-center GPU products. With over 30 years of experience spanning graphics, performance engineering, efficient computing, and security, he specializes in resolving the toughest GPU performance challenges and shaping the methodologies used to set and attain performance goals. He is a named inventor on more than one hundred issued U.S. patents across GPU performance, efficient computing, and platform security.
