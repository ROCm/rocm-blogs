# Mark Granroth Wilding

Mark is an AI Scientist at AMD Silo AI, specializing in Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning applications. At Silo he has worked on a wide variety of applications of NLP to real-world tasks. More recently, he has worked in the Robotics domain, applying machine learning models to Autonomous Driving applications, including 3D scene reconstruction.
His earlier research covered historical newspaper exploration using topic modelling, NLP for low-resource languages, Computational Creativity and computational music processing.
