# Lin Zhao

Lin Zhao is a Senior Member of Technical Staff (SMTS) engineer in the AIG-AIS group at AMD, where he focuses on model optimization and evaluation for deployment on AMD AI accelerators. As a core member of AMD Quark team, he specializes in model compression and contributes to the development of quantization algorithms within Quark. Before joining AMD, he worked on efficient matrix solvers in electronic design automation (EDA) and AI algorithms in advertising. He received his PhD in Computational Mathematics from Chinese Academy of Sciences.
