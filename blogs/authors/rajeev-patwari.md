# Rajeev Patwari

Rajeev Patwari is a Fellow at AMD, where he leads Applied AI and inference optimization across GPU and NPU platforms. He drives the architecture and development of efficient AI inference systems, bringing advances in LLMs, VLMs, and image generation into production. His expertise spans model optimization, inference performance modeling, kernel development, and software development and optimization. He is a named inventor on numerous patents and a co-author of [research publications](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=6BI01aMAAAAJ) on model compression, accuracy recovery, and efficient AI execution on heterogeneous systems.
