# Zongheng Yang

Zongheng Yang is the co-creator and project lead of SkyPilot, an open-source framework that orchestrates AI and batch jobs on any cloud, any cluster—with high infra portability and simplicity. Zongheng holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from UC Berkeley. His dissertation applied AI to accelerate data systems, which was awarded an Honorable Mention for the SIGMOD Jim Gray Dissertation Award. Previously, he worked on scalable AI systems at Google Brain.
