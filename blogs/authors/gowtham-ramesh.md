# Gowtham Ramesh

Gowtham Ramesh is an Applied research engineer at AMD GenAI Team where he works on LLMs. He received his masters degree from University of Wisconsin Madison. Before joining AMD, he worked on multi-hop QA at UW-madison, speech task vectors at Google Research and multilingual models at IIT-Madras.
