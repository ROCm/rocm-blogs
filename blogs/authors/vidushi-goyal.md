Vidushi Goyal is a Member of Technical Staff on AMD’s AI Models and Applications team, where she focuses on optimizing LLM performance and scaling efficiency. She earned her Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Michigan in 2023, with a research emphasis on improving performance and energy efficiency for edge devices. Her broader research interests lie in hardware-software co-design, with the goal of developing end-to-end systems that are optimized for performance, energy consumption, and sustainability.
