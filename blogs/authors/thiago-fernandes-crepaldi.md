# Thiago Fernandes Crepaldi

Thiago is a PMTS Engineer at AMD, specialized in AI model optimization. His work as technical lead for AMD Quark is centered on enhancing inference performance through advanced tooling, quantization algorithms, and High-Performance Computing (HPC). Holding a Master’s degree in Computer Science from the University of Campinas, Thiago combines deep expertise in AI stacks, cloud computing, and software engineering to advance the SOTA solutions for ML.
