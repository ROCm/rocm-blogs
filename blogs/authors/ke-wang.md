# Ke Wang

Ke Wang is a Senior Member of Technical Staff (SMTS) engineer in the AIG-AIS group at AMD. He is primarily responsible for the design and maintenance of user APIs for AMD-Quark, as well as interfaces with downstream frameworks. He also contributes to the research and development of quantization algorithms and is one of the main contributors to the AMD-Quark project. Prior to joining AMD, he played a significant role in Xilinx Vitis-AI platform. Ke holds a master's degree in electrical engineering from Xidian University.
