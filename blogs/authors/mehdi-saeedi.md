# Mehdi Saeedi

Mehdi Saeedi, PhD, is a Principal Member of Technical Staff at AMD, where his research spans reinforcement learning for video games, robotics, and sim-to-real transfer, as well as GPU performance analysis, profiling tools, and LLM-driven kernel optimization for the ROCm ecosystem.
