# Eriko Nurvitadhi

Eriko Nurvitadhi is CAIO and co-founder of MangoBoost, Inc. Now in charge of our entire product organization, he worked on R&D and technology transfers at Intel before joining MangoBoost, Inc. His research area is in computer architecture, with emphasis on hardware accelerator architectures (FPGAs, ASICs) and their ecosystems (systems, software flows) for emerging workloads, such as AI and data analytics. He has over 60 peer-reviewed publications (H-index of 26), ~30 patents granted, and ~50 patents pending in this area. In 2020, he was recognized as one of Intel's top 30 inventors by Intel Patent Group and a Mahboob Khan Outstanding Liaison award by Semiconductor Research Corporation. He has served on program committees of IEEE/ACM conferences, serving as the Technical Program Chair for FCCM 2022. He received a PhD in ECE from Carnegie Mellon University, and an MBA from Oregon State University.
