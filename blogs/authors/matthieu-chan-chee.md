# Matthieu Chan Chee

Matthieu Chan Chee is a Senior AI Engineer at AMD specializing in large language models and computer vision. His work spans LLM-powered code generation solutions deployed globally across AMD, computer vision systems including the AMD Image Inspector, and research presented at VISAPP, FORGE (ICSE), and ICML.

Prior to joining AMD, Matthieu ranked first at the National Mathematics Olympiad, was named one of 37 worldwide recipients of the University of Toronto's full-ride Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship, and developed object detection models as part of the university's autonomous driving team, which won the SAE International AutoDrive Challenge.
