# Matthieu Chan Chee

Matthieu Chan Chee is a Senior AI Engineer at AMD, focusing on LLMs and computer vision. His work includes the AMD Image Inspector among others, and has appeared at the VISAPP, FORGE (ICSE) and ICML conferences/workshops.

Prior to joining AMD, Matthieu had ranked first at the National Mathematics Olympiad of Mauritius, and won the SAE International AutoDrive Challenge as part of the University of Toronto's autonomous driving team.
