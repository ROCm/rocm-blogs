# Sonya Yang

Sonya Yang is an AI Software Development Engineer at AMD. She holds a Master’s degree in Computer Science from National Tsing Hua University and specializes in computer vision, AI model development, and sim-to-real robotics research. Her work centers on building scalable simulation-training pipelines that enable robots to operate more efficiently and safely across virtual and physical environments. She also bridges academic and industry research collaborations both within and outside the company, advancing AMD’s involvement in cutting-edge AI and robotics innovation.
