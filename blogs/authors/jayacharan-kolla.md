Jayacharan Kolla is a Product Manager at AMD, responsible for ROCm Developer Tools and Communications Libraries. They are dedicated to enhancing the ROCm ecosystem, enabling enterprises, researchers, and developers to leverage AMD GPUs for AI, scientific computing, and next-generation workloads. With over six years of experience, they have been building and optimizing HPC and AI solutions for customers across both on-premises and cloud environments. Jayacharan also holds a Master’s degree in Computer Science from Texas Tech University.
