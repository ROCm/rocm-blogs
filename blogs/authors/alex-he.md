# Alex He

Alex He currently serves as a Senior Member of Techincal Staff (SMTS) within AMD's Artificial Intelligence Group. In this role, Alex focuses on expanding AMD's AI solutions and advancing the ROCm technology ecosystem. With a wealth of experience in both data center and edge computing, he is passionate about sharing his expertise with others. As an open-source software enthusiast, Alex has contributed to notable projects such as the OPEA, vLLM and Ollama for ROCm enablement. He also previously contributed codes to the Linux Kernel. Alex hopes to inspire more developers to leverage AMD products in their work. Alex holds a Master’s degree in Software Engineering from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC).
