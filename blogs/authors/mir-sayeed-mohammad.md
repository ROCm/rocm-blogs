# Mir Sayeed Mohammad

Sayeed is a Research Associate in RAD at AMD and a PhD student at Georgia Tech. His research focus is on robotics and computer vision. During his time at AMD he worked on optimizations of vision-language-action models and world-action-models for robot deployment using the AMD Ryzen AI MAX+ 395 computer platform. His PhD focuses on adaptive algorithms for efficient perception targeted toward edge robotics and vision systems.
