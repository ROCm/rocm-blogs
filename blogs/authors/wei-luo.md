# Wei Luo

Wei Luo is a senior manager at AMD, where he focuses on model optimization and inference framework performance for AMD AI accelerators. He brings a deep background in AI systems engineering, model optimization, HPC and EDA. Wei served as the technical lead for AMD Quark, contributing to the development of model compression and optimization techniques that accelerate large-scale AI inference. Prior to joining AMD, he worked on the Xilinx Vitis-AI model optimization toolkit. He holds a PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the Chinese Academy of Sciences and has over a decade of research and development experience.
