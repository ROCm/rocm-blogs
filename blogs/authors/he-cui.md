He Cui is an AI Engineer at AMD’s AIG team, specializing in generative video models. His current work focuses on long video generation as well as optimizing generation quality and speed, and he is one of the key contributors to the Hummingbird model. He earned his Master’s degree from Shandong University in 2023 and has been working at AMD since then. In addition, he has conducted extensive research in low-level vision tasks—such as image and video super-resolution and denoising—as well as in the 3D vision domain.
