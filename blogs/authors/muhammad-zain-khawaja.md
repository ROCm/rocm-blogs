# Muhammad Zain Khawaja

Muhammad Zain Khawaja is a technology entrepreneur, roboticist and AI expert. Zain has architected and published multiple popular open-source AI models for scene perception and autonomous driving. Zain is serving as the President of the Autoware Foundation, a leading Physical AI institution developing open-source technology. He has previously been Founder & CEO of multiple start-ups in the Physical AI space.
