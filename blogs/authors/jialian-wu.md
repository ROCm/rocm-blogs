Jialian Wu is a Research Scientist in the GenAI team at AMD where he works on large language model. He received his PhD degree in computer science and engineering from State University of New York at Buffalo in 2023. His experience during his PhD lies in computer vision and vision-language. He did research internships at Microsoft Azure AI, Amazon, and Horizon Robotics.
