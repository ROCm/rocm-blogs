# Poovaiah Palangappa

Poovaiah is a Principal Member of Technical Staff at AMD, where he focuses on performance optimizations for large language models (LLMs) and recommendation systems on AMD Instinct™ GPUs. As a performance architect on the MLPerf team, he drives workload optimizations, accurate benchmarking, and performance projections. In addition, Poovaiah contributes to thought leadership around inference engines, LLMs, and recommendation systems, helping advance industry understanding of AI performance at scale.
