# Kumar Saurabh

Kumar Saurabh is a Member of Technical Staff in the Server HPC group at AMD.
He holds a PhD in Mechanical Engineering from the Iowa State University. He has a strong background
in high performance scientific computing. He works closely with customers and application teams to
understand real world performance challenges and to help shape accessible,
effective scientific computing workflows for large scale HPC systems using AMD Instinct™ GPUs.
