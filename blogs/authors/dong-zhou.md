# Dong Zhou

Dong Zhou is a Senior Member of Technical Staff in the AI Group of AMD. He leads a team for AI/ML-related Gaming and Creativity models' application, including Image/Video processing/enhancement （especially Neural accelerated rendering） and Image/Video/World Generation. Prior to joining AMD, he worked at Visual Computing Group of ByteDance AI Lab. He received his bachelor's and master’s degree from Tsinghua University.
