# Jiangyong Ren

Jiangyong Ren is a Senior Member of Technical Staff (SMTS) in AIG-AIS group at AMD, where he specializes in inference framework optimization for the company's AI accelerators. His areas of expertise include quantization and compression, GPU performance tuning, and high-performance computing. Previously, Jiangyong was a key contributor to the Quark project, responsible for the research and development of quantization technology.
