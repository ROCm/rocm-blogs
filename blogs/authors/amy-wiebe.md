# Amy Wiebe

She has worked on technical documentation for the Instinct data center GPUs, contributing to ROCm blogs, and supported various ROCm-related initiatives. Amy is interested in graphics rendering, GPU computing, and AI/ML.

Outside of work, she enjoys reading, playing piano, and creating games.
