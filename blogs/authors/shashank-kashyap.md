# Shashank Kashyap

Shashank Kashyap is a SMTS Software Systems Design Engineer in the Compute and Enterprise AI group at AMD with a focus on AI, HPC and Cloud Computing. He holds a Master's degree in Aeronautics and Astronautics from Purdue University. He specializes in extensive performance optimization and characterization of AI/HPC workloads on distributed systems. He also focuses on developing software solutions across Enterprise AI and HPC for AMD server products.
