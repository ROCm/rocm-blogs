# Stanislau Fink

Stanislau Fink is SMTS Machine Learning Engineer in the Enterprise Artificial Intelligence group at AMD, where he works in a team that focuses on building solutions for large-scale AI inference. He holds a Master’s degree in Computer Science from the Georgia Institute of Technology. He has over 7 years of experience in developing and deploying Machine Learning based software solutions.
