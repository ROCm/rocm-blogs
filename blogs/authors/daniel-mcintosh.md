# Daniel Mcintosh

Daniel McIntosh is a Sr. Software Development Engineer at AMD. He is focused on reducing the barrier to entry for GPU programming, with expertise in concurrency, API design and HIP. Daniel holds a Bachelor of Computer Science from the University of Waterloo and has a background in compilers and standard library development.
