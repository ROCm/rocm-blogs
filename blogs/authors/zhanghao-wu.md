# Zhanghao Wu

Zhanghao Wu is the co-creator of SkyPilot and holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from UC Berkeley. He has worked across AI and computer systems and has been recognized with the NSDI 2024 Best Paper Award and the IBM PhD Fellowship. Zhanghao also co-created LMSys, where he helped launch the Vicuna project and Chatbot Arena, influential efforts in the open AI ecosystem. Previously, he worked on efficient AI at MIT and ByteDance.
