# Rahul Biswas

Rahul Biswas is a Senior Member of the Technical Staff in the Software Engineering division at AMD. He has a wide range of interests, including physics, computation, survey science, and AI. He holds a PhD in Physics from UIUC (2010). During his subsequent research in the area of cosmology, he worked on different aspects, such as computational methods for studying large-scale structures, extracting scientific information from extensive astronomical datasets using statistics and AI, and developing simulation and analysis pipelines within teams in large collaborations for a number of astronomy surveys. In the industry, he largely worked in the area of Natural Language Processing building applications using different approaches including traditional machine learning, large language models, and prototypes for agentic systems.
