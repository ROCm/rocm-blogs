# Dylan Lim

Dylan Lim is a Senior Research Scientist at Together AI, where he works on high-performance systems for machine learning. He studied Computer Science at Stanford University, where he was a member of Hazy Research. His previous work includes ML compilers, whole-model kernel fusion, and performant multi-GPU communication primitives.
