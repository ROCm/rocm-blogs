# Adam Chan

Adam is a Product Management intern at AMD and a Computer Science student at the University of Calgary.

At AMD, Adam works closely with the hipBLASLt team, contributing to technical documentation, release planning, process improvements, roadmap development, and product showcases. He also supports the ROCm Technical Blog program, where he has authored and co-authored content for the ROCm Blogs site, with contributions spanning publishing automation, repository migration, and end-to-end release of technical material.

Outside of work, Adam's interests include sailing, Magic: The Gathering, Dungeons & Dragons, and drawing.
