# Huan Li

Huan Li is an MTS Software Development Engineer in AMD’s ML Framework team, specializing in training and inference optimization on AMD architectures. His work focuses on kernel optimization, large-scale benchmarking, and end-to-end performance optimization for recommendation, ranking, and large language models on AMD Instinct GPUs.
