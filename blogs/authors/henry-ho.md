Henry Ho holds an M.S. in Electronic Engineering from National Taiwan University. He worked on developing OpenCL-based AI inference accelerators on FPGAs for five years before joining AMD. Currently at AMD, he leads the optimization of the GEMM assembly kernels for cutting-edge hardware architecture, develops GEMM kernel selection algorithms, and supports customers including Microsoft and Phantom.
