# Dominic Widdows

Dominic is a Principal Deep Learning Data Scientist at AMD, where he works on adapting and optimizing
AI models on AMD hardware. Dominic specializes in software engineering for advanced mathematics,
with a focus on high-dimensional vector and tensor representations, including many applications
in language modeling, quantum computing, and AMDs core AI platforms for deep learning and inference.
Dominic holds a DPhil in differential geometry from the University of Oxford.
