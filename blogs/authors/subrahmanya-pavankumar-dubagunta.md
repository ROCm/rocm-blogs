# Subrahmanya Pavankumar Dubagunta

S. Pavankumar Dubagunta works on optimizing deep learning kernels using LLM agents at AMD. Prior to this, he worked at Amazon on improving LLM-based speech-to-speech models, and led innovation in speech and conversational AI solutions at Uniphore, focusing on end-to-end speech recognition, personalization, and related areas. His experience spans innovation, engineering, and taking projects to production. He holds a PhD degree from École polytechnique fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL) and a master's degree from Indian Institute of Technology Madras. He is passionate about all aspects of speech and language processing.
