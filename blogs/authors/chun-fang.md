# Chun Fang

Chun Fang is a SMTS in the Artificial Intelligence group at AMD, where he focuses on LLM inference benchmarking, performance analysis, and ecosystem enablement for AMD GPUs. He holds a PhD in Applied Mathematics from the University of Helsinki. His interests include LLM serving, distributed inference, ROCm software, benchmarking, and open-source AI systems.
