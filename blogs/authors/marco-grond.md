Marco is a Software Product Manager at AMD responsible for the Open Source ROCm expansion toolkits tailored for industry solutions.

With a PhD in Computer Science from the University of Calgary and a Master's in Applied Mathematics from Stellenbosch University in South Africa, Marco brings a wealth of knowledge and expertise to his role. His background experience includes developing innovative solutions for real-world challenges such as object tracking, emergency response preparedness, and the early detection and prevention of infrastructure failures in the energy sector. Marco's work exemplifies his commitment to advancing technology through addressing critical industry needs.
