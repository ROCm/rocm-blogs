# John Tyler

John is a Software Development Co-op student, currently finishing his Undergraduate degree in Computing Science at the University of Alberta.

John first joined AMD to apply his experience in numerical methods and GPU programming on the rocSOLVER team to bring GPU accelerated linear algebra operations to ROCm. He enhanced the build system, benchmarking and testing suite, assisted in validating and debugging on new or emulated hardware, and assisted in the optimization of SYEVD by profiling the routine and benchmarking changes. More recently, John joined the ROCm Storage team to expand AMD's software suite for accelerated data transfer between GPUs and storage devices. He enhanced hipFile's reliability and platform compatibility, improved testing by focusing on tests that stress or exercise the GPU memory model, and led the development of CI for rocm-aic.

His interests include computer architecture, graphics, multimedia, and high-performance computing. Outside of his work at AMD, John enjoys spending his time baking, skiing, winding down with some yoga, and occasionally contributing to open source software.
