# Chao Chen

Chao Chen is a Principal Member of Technical Staff on AMD’s ML Frameworks team. His work focuses on low-latency online recommendation inference, large-scale foundation model pre-training and post-training, reinforcement learning training, and generative AI inference across text, image, and multimodal workloads.

His technical scope spans ML frameworks, compilers, runtimes, communication libraries, and GPU kernels, with an emphasis on feature enablement and end-to-end performance optimization for AI workloads on AMD hardware. He holds a Ph.D. and an M.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Warwick, with a research focus on parallel and distributed computing and high-performance computing (HPC).
