# Vinayak Gokhale

Vinayak Gokhale is a software engineer at AMD and the lead architect of the AITER-Triton backend. He leads a team developing and optimizing high-performance Triton and Gluon kernels for [AITER](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter), AMD's ML operators library. His work spans the full stack, from kernels to production serving, integrating AMD's software with LLM frameworks including ATOM, vLLM, and SGLang. These kernels deliver out-of-the-box performance across a wide range of frontier models.
