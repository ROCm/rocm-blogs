# Babak Poursartip

Babak Poursartip is a software developer and team lead in hipBLASLt team. He has a background in parallel computing, computational mechanics, and optimization for various applications. Currently at AMD, he is leading the GEMM optimization team, where he focuses on enhancing GEMM kernels to improve the performance of various machine learning models.
