# Yuchen Lin

Yuchen Lin is currently a Senior Member of Technical Staff on AMD’s Data Center GPU team, specializing in AI system-level performance tuning and optimization, with a focus on the ROCm software stack integration. He received his Ph.D. and B.S. degrees in Computer Science from National Taiwan University in 2021 and 2015. His areas of expertise include AI performance optimization, efficient deep learning, and operating systems.
