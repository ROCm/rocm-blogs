Clement Lin is a Senior Member of Technical Staff on AMD’s Data Center GPU team. He is interested in technologies that advance the ROCm ecosystem, focusing on GPU computing for AI workloads and large language models (LLMs), as well as system-level performance tuning. Clement holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from National Tsing Hua University and most recently worked as a senior engineer at MediaTek before joining AMD.
