# Ivan Tikhonov

Ivan Tikhonov is a Member of Technical Staff (MTS) at AMD, where he focuses on the computer vision inference flow on AMD GPUs and on LLM inference optimization. Before joining AMD, Ivan spent over six years on Intel's OpenVINO toolkit, most recently as a Senior AI Frameworks Engineer and Team Lead, leading graph-optimization work for AI model inference across CPU, GPU, and NPU backends. He began his career as a Graphics Software Engineer at Intel, contributing to the Intel Graphics Performance Analyzers (GPA) profiling toolchain.
