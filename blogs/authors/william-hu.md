# William Hu

William Hu is a Member of Technical Staff (MTS) at Modal. He received his M.S. in Computer Science from Stanford University, where he developed HipKittens and was a contributor to the KernelBench project. His work focuses on understanding how hardware and software co-design can unlock new capabilities in efficient AI computation.
