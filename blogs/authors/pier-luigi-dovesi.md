# Pier Luigi Dovesi

Pier Luigi Dovesi is a Senior Member of the Technical Staff at AMD Silo AI, focusing on multimodal and generative AI. His extensive work in perception and safety for autonomous vehicles and robots originated the idea that AIs – just like humans – should continuously adapt. This insight led to the development of novel methods that enable AI to learn and improve independently and continuously, even in unfamiliar and complex environments.

Currently, Pier is directing projects including 3D scene reconstruction, data engines, and multimodal reasoning. His regular contributions to AI research include publications in 3DV, ICRA, ECCV, ICCV, BMVC, and CVPR, collaborating with top-tier institutions including KTH Royal Institute of Technology, the University of Bologna, La Sapienza, UniMoRE, ETH Zurich, and TU Munich.

As an active member of the AI community, Pier contributes to knowledge sharing and promotes diversity through public speaking, organizing educational events, mentoring, and advancing humanitarian initiatives with AI.
