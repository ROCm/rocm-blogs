# Huidong Ji

Huidong Ji is an intern in AMD Quark team, focusing on low-bit LLM quantization and deployment, with a particular emphasis on FP4 formats. He is pursuing a Ph.D. in Electronic Information at Fudan University and holds a Master's degree in Mechatronics from the University of Siegen. His research interests include model compression, efficient LLM inference, and AI systems optimization.
