# Saroosh Shabbir

Saroosh Shabbir is a Senior Member of Technical Staff at AMD Silo AI. As part of the Enterprise AI division, Saroosh contributes towards building reliable and scalable AI solutions for enterprises across industries. The Enterprise AI platform provides computer vision and large language model solutions, with additional verticals in development.

Saroosh holds a PhD in Quantum Optics from KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Stockholm, and has worked as a machine learning engineer in the industry ever since.
