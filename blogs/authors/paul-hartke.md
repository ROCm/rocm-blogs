# Paul Hartke

Paul Hartke is a Fellow in AMD Research and Advanced Development (ARAD).  Prior to the AMD acquisition of Xilinx in 2022, he was a member of the Xilinx Chief Technology Office which had responsibility for advanced technology development across the Xilinx adaptive computing portfolio.  His pathfinding interest is the intersection of compute, networking, and storage spanning from embedded systems to data center architectures.
