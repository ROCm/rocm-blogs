# Christian Gilli

Christian is a Senior Member of Technical Staff in the Artificial Intelligence group at AMD specializing in GPU performance and inference optimization. He has a strong background in GPU programming and inference of Large Language Models across the full stack, from low-level kernel optimization to high-level deployment frameworks. He holds a Master's degree in Computer Science from the University of Modena and Reggio-Emilia.
