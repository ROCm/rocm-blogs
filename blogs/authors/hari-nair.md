# Hari Nair

Hari Nair is a Senior Software Systems Designer in the Compute and Enterprise AI Group at AMD. His interest lies in optimizing performance of Generative AI Models and developing leading-edge solutions for data center AI deployments in the industry. He holds a Master's Degree in Electrical Engineering from Columbia University with a specialization in Data-driven Analysis and Computation.
