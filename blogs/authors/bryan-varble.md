# Bryan Varble

Bryan Varble brings over 34 years of experience as a software engineer and system designer, specializing in real-time, mission-critical, and high-speed communication systems. His expertise spans the design of ATM, Ethernet, Fibre Channel, SS7 voice, and H.248/SIP voice/data switches and routers. Bryan has led solution designs for major initiatives including Sprint/LTI H.248 with enhanced 911 support (U.S. Patent #US-7218723-B2), Verizon FiOS Video, AT&T U-Verse targeted advertising, and high-performance computing (HPC) clusters at NASA Ames and beyond.
