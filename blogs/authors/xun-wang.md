# Xun Wang

Xun Wang is a Member of Technical Staff (MTS) Product Application Engineer at AMD. He focuses on developing high-performance operators across heterogeneous backends, optimizing models through quantization techniques and building high-performance software solutions. Passionate about artificial intelligence and the evolving machine learning ecosystem, Xun helps developers harness a wide range of frameworks, libraries, and tools to build innovative AI experiences on the ROCm platform.He holds his Master's degree from Zhejiang University.
