# Anshu Raina

Anshu Raina is an AI Performance Engineer on the Training at Scale team at AMD, where he focuses on performance modeling and optimization for large-scale AI training on AMD GPUs. With prior experience at Intel and NVIDIA, he brings a broad perspective across the GPU and accelerator landscape. Anshu holds a Master's in Computer Science from the University of Wisconsin-Madison and a Bachelor's from NIT Rourkela, India. He is passionate about hardware-software co-design and pushing the boundaries of end-to-end LLM training and inference performance.
