Chao Li is an SMTS engineer in AIG-AIS group at AMD. He leads a team focusing on model quantization algorithms and tool development, especially for AMD Quark ONNX quantization, and provides support for these models and techniques to key customers on AMD hardware. His research interests include model optimization algorithms, inference acceleration, and computer vision. He received his Master’s degree in Computer Science from Peking University.
