# Kaiping Lu

Kaiping Lu is a Member of Technical Staff on AMD's Data Center GPU team. His work focuses on multi-node heterogeneous system integration within the AMD ROCm ecosystem, with emphasis on enabling and debugging large-scale GPU clusters and RCCL. He is responsible for system-level integration across compute, networking, and GPU components, ensuring optimal performance for AI workloads through comprehensive system tuning and troubleshooting.
