# Sukriti Choudhary

Sukriti Choudhary is a Senior Member of Technical Staff at AMD, where she leads the development and optimization of advanced AI software stacks for AMD GPUs. With extensive expertise in real-time systems, embedded software, and heterogeneous computing, she is deeply passionate about advancing AI, machine learning, and data science through sophisticated and efficient hardware-software integration.
