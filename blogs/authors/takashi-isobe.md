Takashi Isobe is a Research Scientist on the AIG-MODELS team at AMD, where he leads projects on video generative models. His primary research focuses on improving training efficiency, and he also works on agent generative models. Before joining AMD, he was a Research Scientist at Kuaishou and Xiaohongshu. He earned his master’s degree from Tsinghua University in 2021, graduating as an Outstanding Graduate. As first author, he has published 8 papers at top-tier conferences, such as CVPR, ICCV, and ECCV. Since 2020, he has served as a reviewer for leading AI conferences, including ICLR, IJCAI, AAAI, CVPR, ICCV, BMVC, WACV, and ACM MM.
