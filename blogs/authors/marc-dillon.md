# Marc Dillon

Marc Dillon is an experienced technology professional currently serving as Lead AI Engineer at AMD since January 2025. Prior to this role, Marc held positions at Eficode as a Principal Consultant, focusing on transformative strategies for software development and operational efficiencies. As Chief Technology Officer at Basemark, Marc emphasized leadership development for GPU benchmarking and autonomous drive software. Additional roles include Vice President at ZenRobotics Ltd., overseeing software, programs, service, and testing for robotic waste management.
