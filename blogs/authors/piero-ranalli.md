# Piero Ranalli

Piero Ranalli is a senior AI scientist and senior member of the technical staff at AMD Silo AI, with a focus on physics, machine learning and advanced analytics. He has extensive experience on computer vision, mathematical modelling and generative AI, stemming from a his experience in consulting for a variety of industries and a long academic career in astrophysics.
Piero's current focus is on robotics, including 3D reconstruction of scenes and objects, and on computer vision for the life sciences.
