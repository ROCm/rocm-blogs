# Jianghui Wang

Jianghui is a senior AI developer in the AI Group at AMD. Her current interests include AI agents and embodied AI. She hold a Master’s degree from the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) in Germany, where she specialized in machine learning and robotics. Jianghui has worked at Mobis R&D and Li Auto.
