# Ognjen Plavsic

Ognjen Plavsic is a Member of Technical Staff at AMD on the core Triton team, specializing in GPU compilers and high-performance computing. He works across the hardware–software boundary on GPU kernel optimization and performance analysis, with a particular focus on accelerating machine learning workloads across the various AMD Instinct architectures.
