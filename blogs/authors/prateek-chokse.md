# Prateek Chokse

Prateek is a Software Engineer in the Domains team, focused on high-performance computing and GPU-accelerated systems. At AMD, he develops and enables scalable solutions that leverage AMD GPUs, working across the stack to translate hardware capabilities into efficient, real-world performance through optimized libraries and system-level design. His work involves performance tuning, scalability optimization, and ensuring that GPU technologies are effectively applied to modern compute workloads.
He also brings prior experience in architecting and building scalable, multi-component inference systems, including distributed services, worker pipelines, and backend components. He has deployed and managed these solutions across both cloud and on-premise environments using Kubernetes, enabling reliable and efficient execution at scale.
