# Jason Jia

Jason Xijie Jia is a Senior Manager for Software Development in AIG. His career focuses on hardware acceleration, especially on CV and AI domain. Recent years Jason team is working on RyzenAI NPU support and optimization, including kernel, tiling, and delivery, especially supporting ImageGen app such as Stable-Diffusion models. Jason received his M.S. degree from Tsinghua University, Beijing, China, in 2016.
