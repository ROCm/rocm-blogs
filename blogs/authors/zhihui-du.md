# Zhihui Du

Zhihui Du is a Principal Member of Technical Staff at AMD, where he works on GPU kernel performance and numerical reproducibility across the ROCm™ software stack. His recent work spans Triton and HIP kernels for AI and scientific workloads, including the triangle operators at the core of AlphaFold-style protein structure prediction models, along with performance and determinism investigations across PyTorch, RCCL, hipBLASLt, and MIGraphX on AMD Instinct™ GPUs. He holds a PhD in computer science and has published widely in parallel and high-performance computing.
