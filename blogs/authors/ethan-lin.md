Ethan Lin holds an M.S. in Computer Science from National Tsinghua University. He worked on developing 3D rendering and had experience on building cross-platform in-house rendering engines for eight years. He also worked on developing AR with 3D reconstruction on Vive for 2 years before joining AMD. Currently at AMD, he works on hipBLASLt library, including developing the GEMM assembly kernels for cutting-edge hardware architecture, and GEMM kernel selection algorithms.
