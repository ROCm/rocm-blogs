# Jan Stephan

Jan Stephan is a Senior Software Development Engineer in the AI Group at AMD. With almost fifteen years of experience in GPU software development, his work has spanned simulation, computed tomography (CT) reconstruction, performance analysis, parallel algorithms, and high-performance computing. He holds a Diploma in Computer Science from TU Dresden and maintains a longstanding interest in advancing the capabilities of modern computing systems.
