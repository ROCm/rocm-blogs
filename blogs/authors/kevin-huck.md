# Kevin Huck

Kevin Huck is a Principal Member of Technical Staff (PMTS) Software Applications Engineer in the HPC and Sovereign AI Enablement group at AMD. He earned his PhD in Computer and Information Science at the University of Oregon. He has over two decades of experience developing performance measurement and monitoring tools for HPC applications and in optimizing those applications. At AMD he supports customers with correctness and optimization challenges in scientific applications running on large-scale HPC clusters with AMD Instinct™ GPUs.
