# Edwin Lim

Edwin Lim is a AI Systems Software Engineer at MangoBoost, where he builds optimized software to accelerate AI workload performance on the latest AMD Instinct GPUs. He focuses on pushing the performance of modern LLM models through careful system bottleneck analysis and a deep understanding of the technology stack. He obtained a B.S. in EECS from the University of California, Berkeley, and recently an M.S. in ECE from Carnegie Mellon University, where he was advised by professor James C. Hoe.
