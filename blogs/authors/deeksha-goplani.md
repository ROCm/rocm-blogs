# Deeksha Goplani

Deeksha is a Senior Member of Technical Staff (SMTS) at AMD, driving the open-source ROCm stack to accelerate AI workloads on AMD Instinct GPUs.

She is a seasoned AI professional with 11 years of experience specializing in High Performance Computing and AI acceleration. She has contributed to the development and optimization of open-source AI software stacks for ARM CPUs, including OpenBLAS and XGBoost, and has built end-to-end AI workloads for x86 CPUs.
