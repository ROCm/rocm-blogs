# Baiqiang XIA

Baiqiang holds a PhD in 3D face recognition and has over 14 years of experience in the AI domain. He currently leads the Model Tooling team in Finland, focusing on AI4Science applications in HPC — such as AI-based weather forecasting—and visual media, including video generation and editing. Previously, he built and directed R&D teams developing backend AI models for image and video search engines, and drove large-scale projects such as training CLIP, video generation, and large multimodal models. He has received a Best Paper award at an international conference and secured 1st place in an industrial competition.
