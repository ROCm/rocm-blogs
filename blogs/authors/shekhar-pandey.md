# Shekhar Pandey

Shekhar Pandey is an  AI Solutions Architect at AMD with a Master’s degree in Computer Engineering. He specializes in AI infrastructure, large language models (LLMs), and deep learning optimizations for high-performance computing. Shekhar has made notable open-source contributions, including work on llm.c, demonstrating his expertise in LLM inference and AI acceleration.
Beyond his professional work, Shekhar is an avid problem solver with a strong presence on LeetCode, where he actively tackles complex algorithmic challenges and contributes to the problem-solving community. His passion for data structures, algorithms, and competitive programming fuels his drive to innovate in AI and machine learning.
