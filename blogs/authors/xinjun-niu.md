# Xinjun Niu

Xinjun Niu is a PMTS engineer in AMD’s AIG-AIS group, specializing in AI model optimization for inference, tooling, quantization algorithms, HPC, and SPICE simulation. He is the main creator of AMD-Quark, a tool powering Ryzen AI, MIGPU, and ZenAI, and also the lead developer of the Xilinx VitisAI quantizer. With a master’s degree in Electrical Engineering from Xidian University, he brings deep expertise in bridging hardware and AI software, advancing cutting-edge solutions in model efficiency and deployment.
