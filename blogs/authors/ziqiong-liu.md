# Ziqiong Liu

Ziqiong Liu is a senior engineer in the AI group of AMD. She leads a team for AIGC optimization and application, including inference acceleration, code generation agent, recommender system and efficient reasoning model. Prior to joining AMD, she worked at Alibaba and Xiaohongshu. She received her PhD from Tsinghua University.
