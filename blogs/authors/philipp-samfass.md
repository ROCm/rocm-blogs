# Philipp Samfass

Philipp Samfass is a Senior Member of Technical Staff (SMTS) and Software Development Engineer in AMD’s AI Open-Source Software (AIOSS) group. His current work focuses on expanding AMD’s AI software ecosystem, including the development of ROCm Data Science (ROCm-DS) components such as hipDF and several of its supporting lower-level libraries. 

Before joining AMD, Philipp earned a doctorate (Dr. rer. nat., PhD-equivalent) in Computer Science from the Technical University of Munich (TUM), where he specialized in parallel computing. He is deeply passionate about harnessing cutting-edge modern computing hardware to solve challenges in advanced computing and data sciences with a strong focus on performance.
