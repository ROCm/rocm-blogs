# Jingai Yu

Jingai Yu is an AI Engineer at AMD's AIG team. Her current work focuses on image generation models, including improving image quality and accelerating generation process through pruning and distillation, and some applications such as image customization. She received her master's degree from the Ocean University of China in 2015 and has been working at AMD since 2023.
