# Devang Patel

Devang Patel is a GPU Cluster Network Architect at AMD, where he designs large-scale AI/ML clusters. Drawing on prior experience at hyperscalers, service provider, enterprise and network vendor, he brings a broad perspective on networking at scale. Devang holds a graduate degree in Electrical and Communication Engineering and carries 3×JNCIE and 1×CCIE certifications.
