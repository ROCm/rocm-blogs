# Maria Garzaran

Maria Garzaran is a Fellow at AMD, where she focuses on optimizing collective communication and shaping future silicon architecture. She has many years of experience working on highly scalable communication runtimes. Previously a faculty member at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, Maria is a widely recognized expert in parallel programming and architectures, with over 70 publications in top-tier conferences. She is actively involved in conference panels, organization, and program committees, and is an ACM Senior Member and IEEE Member.
