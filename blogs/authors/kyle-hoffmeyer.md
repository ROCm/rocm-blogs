# Kyle Hoffmeyer

Kyle is a software engineer on the AI Models Performance & Optimization team at AMD. His focus is on developing tools to identify and analyze performance bottlenecks in AI workloads on AMD's Instinct GPUs. Before joining full time, Kyle started at AMD as an intern focusing on power-efficient AI computing. Kyle holds a Bachelors in Data Science from the University of Michigan.
