Vinay Joshi is a Research Scientist on the GenAI team at AMD. He obtained his Ph.D. from King's College London and masters from Indian Institute of Technology Bombay. His expertise lie in optimizing AI model architectures for training and inference. A major focus of his research is producing efficient Gen AI models using various optimization techniques, including using AI to optimize AI. He has worked on GPU kernel generation using LLMs, KV-cache optimization, optimizing DLRM’s large embedding tables access patterns, and accurate inference of CNNs on phase-change memory hardware.
