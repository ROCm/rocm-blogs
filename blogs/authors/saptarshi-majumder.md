# Saptarshi Majumder

Saptarshi Majumder is a Research Engineer on the GenAI team at AMD. He works on large language models and is particularly interested in reasoning capabilities for multimodal and vision-language models. Prior to AMD, he was an Applied Scientist at Flipkart’s Foundational Model Team (FMT), where he focused on building diffusion models in the e-commerce domain. He earned his master’s degree from the Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay.
