# Sean Siddens

Sean Siddens is a Senior Software Engineer at AMD Research, where he focuses on kernel optimization for AMD Instinct GPUs. He is also pursuing his PhD in Computer Science at the University of Washington. Sean contributed to HipKittens, a tile-based programming framework for Instinct GPUs, and works on using layout abstractions to improve the performance of GPU kernels.
