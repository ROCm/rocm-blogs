# Pauli Pihajoki

Pauli is a Senior Member of Technical Staff at AMD, focused on both theoretical and applied research tasks. His background is in theoretical astrophysics, with more than 20 years of experience in measurement hardware, bare metal, low and high level coding, algorithm development, computational geometry, radiation transfer, probability theory and machine learning.
