# Paul dos Santos

Paul dos Santos is a Senior Member of Technical Staff (SMTS) at AMD, where he focuses on making advanced AI workloads run efficiently in practice. Over more than a decade, he has worked across data science, machine learning systems, multimodal AI, computer vision, edge inference, and large-scale data platforms. Before AMD, Paul led AI and R&D teams, building and deploying AI systems from early research through production.
