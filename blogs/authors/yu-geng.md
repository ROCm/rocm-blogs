# Yu Geng

Yu Geng is a Member of Technical Staff at AMD’s AIG team, specializing in visual perception and generative visual models. His current work focuses on generative world model. Prior to joining AMD, he worked as perception algorithm engineer at Megvii technology. He received his master’s degree from Tianjin University.
