# Aleksa Arsic

Aleksa Arsic is a Software Engineer at AMD specializing in AI/ML workload optimization across the ROCm stack. His work focuses on cross-stack performance, from ML frameworks down to hardware, identifying bottlenecks and improving system-level efficiency. He has a background in embedded systems and software architecture, with a Master’s degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering.
