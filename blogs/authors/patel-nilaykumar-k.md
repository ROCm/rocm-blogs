# Patel, Nilaykumar K

Nilaykumar Patel is a Senior Member of Technical Staff at AMD, where he designs and develops GPU-accelerated kernel libraries on AMD Instinct GPUs. He specializes in optimizing AI kernels for CNNs and LLMs using tiling, fusion, and parallelization techniques that serve as targets for ML libraries/compilers. Nilaykumar has over a decade of experience in AI acceleration, kernel development, and high-performance computing.
