# Alessio Tonioni

Dr. Alessio Tonioni is a Staff Research Scientist at Google Zurich, where he leads an applied research team working on multimodal machine learning and Multimodal Large Language Models for several Google products. His research activity focuses on several areas around multimodal understanding, generative models, 3D computer vision, and visual text understanding.
