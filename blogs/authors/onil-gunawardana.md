# Onil Gunawardana

Onil is the Director of Product Management for AMD ROCm AI Inference Software. He leads teams focused on cutting edge large scale inference through open source software.

Before AMD, he led machine learning enterprise software product teams at Google and Snowflake. Onil is an alum of Harvard Business School, Stanford Electrical Engineering and Yale. He loves swimming.
