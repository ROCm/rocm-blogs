# Wensong Chan

Wensong Chan is a Member of Technical Staff at AMD’s AIG team, specializing in neural rendering and generative visual models. His current work focuses on ML-based rendering and generative world model. Prior to joining AMD, he worked as a machine learning engineer at Baidu. He received his master’s degree from Xi'an Jiaotong University.
