Meena Arunachalam is a Fellow and Director at AMD, where she leads a team working on AI workload performance for AMD Instinct™ GPUs. Her focus includes optimizing end-to-end performance for data center inference and training, spanning MLPerf benchmarks and open-source GenAI models. She has authored over 20 technical papers and has contributed to strategies that improve the efficiency of AI systems. Meena also shares her perspectives on AI performance and optimization at industry conferences through invited talks and panels.
