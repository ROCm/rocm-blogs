# Jarkko Lehtiranta

Jarkko is a Member of Technical Staff at AMD Silo AI with long software engineering and data science background. He is working with AI engineering and performance optimization teams and his work has included developing production systems in various fields from traditional machine learning applications to RAG applications using LLMs and performance optimizations for diffusion models. He has also contributed to open-source projects like TorchServe. He holds a master's degree in Data Science from University of Helsinki.
