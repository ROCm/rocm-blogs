Joe Shajrawi is a Senior Manager in the Machine Learning frameworks group at AMD. He joined AMD two years ago after kick-starting Meta's first generation silicon targeting their recommendation systems and architecting the KNYFE Domain-Specific Language. He previously held compiler engineering roles at Apple and IBM Research. He lives in Austin, Texas.
