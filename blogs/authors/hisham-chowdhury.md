Hisham Chowdhury is a Fellow within GPU Technology and Engineering at AMD and leads AI and machine learning architecture within the AMD Radeon Software organization. He is at the forefront of developing advanced ML technologies tailored for Radeon GPUs, helping deliver high-performance AI solutions that run seamlessly on AMD hardware. Beyond engineering, Hisham plays a key role in shaping the broader AMD AI strategy, working closely with internal teams and external partners to unify machine learning efforts across the company's consumer product lines. His passion lies in pushing the boundaries of what's possible with AI on GPUs.
