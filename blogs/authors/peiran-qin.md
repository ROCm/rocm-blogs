# Peiran Qin

Peiran Qin is an AI Software Engineer at MangoBoost, where he specializes in LLM inference optimization. He has focused on optimizing inference performance on AMD GPUs for InferenceX and multiple rounds of MLPerf benchmarking. His work centers on vLLM serving and kernel optimizations for the latest large language models, including DeepSeek, Kimi, and GLM. He obtained a pre-doctoral M.S. from the University of Chicago, where he was advised by Professor Haryadi S. Gunawi.
