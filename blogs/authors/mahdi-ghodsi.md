# Mahdi Ghodsi

Mahdi Ghodsi is an AI Solution Architect at AMD, specializing in the integration of open-source AI projects and the development of advanced AI solutions optimized for AMD GPUs. Previously, he led the deployment of production AI systems for dozens of large companies worldwide, ensuring robust and scalable implementations. Mahdi holds a master’s degree from the University of British Columbia.
