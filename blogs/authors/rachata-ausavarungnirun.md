# Rachata Ausavarungnirun

Rachata Ausavarungnirun is the Head of the AI System Architecture Group at MangoBoost, leading the end-to-end R&D of next-generation, full-stack AI architectures. His work focuses on hardware-software co-design to optimize AI software, compute (GPUs, NPUs), multi-tier storage, and networking using FPGA-based DPUs to maximize resource efficiency, performance, and TCO. Prior to this, Rachata was in academia, with research spanning multiple areas including GPU architectures, management of GPUs in the cloud, virtual memory, heterogeneous CPU-GPU architectures, memory subsystems, persistent memory, accelerator designs for bioinformatics applications, and network-on-chip. He has published over 20 papers in premier computer architecture conferences, served on program committees for IEEE/ACM conferences, and is recognized in the ASPLOS Hall of Fame.
