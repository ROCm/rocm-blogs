# Hongyi Yao

Hongyi Yao is a member of Xuanwu Yin’s model optimization team at AMD, focusing on advancing model compression for large-scale AI systems, especially on model quantization. He works on cutting-edge quantization techniques for large language models, vision-language models, and generative image/video pipelines, enabling efficient training and inference across diverse hardware platforms.
