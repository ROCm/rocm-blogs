# Akhila Yeruva

Akhila Yeruva is a Member of Technical Staff in AMD’s DCGPU organization, specializing in designing and validating GPU‑accelerated platforms for AI, HPC, and enterprise workloads. She works closely with customers, engineering, and product teams to architect end‑to‑end solutions involving Kubernetes, ROCm, GPU Operators, Slurm, and large‑scale cluster deployments.

She brings extensive experience across infrastructure design, cluster lifecycle management, and performance‑driven GPU enablement, contributing to platform validation, custom OS image pipelines, observability tooling, and operational best practices for AI/ML environments. Her focus areas include AI infrastructure, GPU scheduling and telemetry, cloud‑native orchestration, and production‑grade platform engineering, helping customers accelerate and operationalize workloads on AMD Instinct™ GPUs.
