# Aku Rouhe

Aku Rouhe (Ph.D.) works on LLM fine-tuning in an Enterprise context as a Senior Member of Technical Staff at AMD Silo AI.

He has been working on speech and natural language technology for ten years, driven by a fascination with language: in his free time, he writes prose and lyrics. During his doctorate at Aalto University (Finland) Aku focused on Speech Recognition, including an internship at the Mila research lab as a core developer of the open source SpeechBrain toolkit.
