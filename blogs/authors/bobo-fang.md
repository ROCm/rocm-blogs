# Bobo Fang

Bobo Fang is a Senior Member of the Technical Staff on AMD’s Data Center GPU team, having joined AMD in January 2025. Prior to that, she worked at MediaTek, where she maintained a continuous integration system for the daily release of an AI compiler SDK. Currently, she focuses on the VLLM framework, including performance and accuracy testing, and is actively exploring topics related to profiling.
