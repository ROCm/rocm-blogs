# Johanna Yang

Johanna Yang is a Member of Technical Staff (MTS) Software Development Engineer at AMD. She focuses on optimizing and deploying generative AI workloads on AMD hardware, with expertise in fine-tuning, inference, and serving large language models (LLMs) and video generation models. Johanna holds a PhD in Bioinformatics and has a consulting background supporting customers across diverse industries, with experience in computer vision, fraud detection, recommender systems, customer feedback LLMs, and reinforcement learning.
