# Matt Qin

Matt Qin is Principal Member of Technical Staff at AMD. With a Ph.D. in Theoretical Physics, his expertise centers on developing advanced ML solutions, including efficient model architectures, high-performance inference systems, multimodal, and computer vision. Alongside model development, he also has proven track record of building robust ML infra to support the full AI lifecycle, from training and evaluation to deployment at scale.
