# Vansh Bhatia

Vansh Bhatia is a Software Development Engineer 2 on AMD's Efficient GenAI team, where he works with customers and conducts research across leading AI topics. His focus areas include training and inference at scale, hybrid models, speculative decoding, and multimodal models. He holds a Master of Science in Applied Computing from the University of Toronto and is based in Markham, Canada.
