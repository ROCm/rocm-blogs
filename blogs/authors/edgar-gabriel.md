# Edgar Gabriel

Edgar Gabriel is a Principal Member of Technical Staff (PMTS) software engineer at AMD. His work is focusing on enhancing the support and performance of communication libraries such as rocSHMEM, UCX, and Open MPI on AMD Instinct and Radeon Accelerators. Prior to joining AMD, he was working for 16 years as a faculty in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Houston. His technical interests are in Communication Libraries, Parallel File I/O, High Performance Computing, and Performance Tuning. He obtained his PhD from the University of Stuttgart, Germany
