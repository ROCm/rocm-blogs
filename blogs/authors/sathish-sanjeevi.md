# Sathish Sanjeevi

Sathish Sanjeevi is an AI software engineer at AMD. His interests include profiling and optimizing GPU kernels. As a performance lead, he has optimized several ML models, resulting in successful MLPerf submissions. Sathish holds a PhD from Delft University of Technology in the Netherlands and a master's degree from RWTH Aachen University in Germany.
