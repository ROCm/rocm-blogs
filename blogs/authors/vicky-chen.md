# Vicky Chen

Vicky Chen is a Senior Software Engineer at AMD Research. Her research interests include kernel optimization across FPGA and GPU platforms, as well as code generation techniques for high-level synthesis (HLS). In addition, through her role in the AMD University Program, she collaborates with leading research centers and universities to deliver academic solutions and support joint research initiatives.
