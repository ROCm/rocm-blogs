Yu Wang is a Principal AI Software Solution Architect at AMD, leading enterprise AI software product management in the Compute and Enterprise AI (CEAI) Business Unit. She drives the product roadmap for large-scale inference, orchestration, and production deployment on AMD Instinct GPUs, enabling scalable, open, and enterprise-ready AI solutions.

Previously, in AMD Artificial Intelligence Group (AIG), she focused on advancing AMD AI software ecosystem and open platform initiatives. Before AMD, she led enablement of NVIDIA GPUs and advanced compute workloads for VMware Cloud Foundation at VMware by Broadcom and launched the first VMware enterprise GenAI offering, Private AI Foundation with NVIDIA. Her background in real-time computer graphics, GPGPU, and HPC provides the technical foundation for designing practical, production-grade AI infrastructure.
