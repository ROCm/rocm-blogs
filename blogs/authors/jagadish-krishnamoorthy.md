# Jagadish Krishnamoorthy

Jagadish Krishnamoorthy is a Senior Member of Technical Staff at AMD, focused on ROCm enablement and GPU acceleration in PyTorch. He contributes to the PyTorch ecosystem, driving features such as low-precision formats (MX FP4/FP8) and grouped GEMM for Mixture-of-Experts inference, integrating high-performance AMD GPU libraries into the PyTorch stack.
