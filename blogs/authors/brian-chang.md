Brian Chang has been a working in AMD since 2022, initially contributing his expertise to the Display Driver team. Currently, he is part of the Data Center GPU team, where his primary focus is on GPU health monitoring. His work involves the development and enhancement of performance monitoring tools, error recovery systems, and profiler metrics to ensure the optimal functionality and reliability of GPUs. In addition to his core responsibilities, Brian is actively involved in the deployment of ROCm/DKMS, working on driver and OS integration to streamline processes and improve system efficiency.
