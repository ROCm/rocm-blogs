# Shao-Chun Lee

Shao-Chun Lee is a software engineer at AMD and the developer and maintainer of AITER-Triton backend. He is responsible of the development and the optimization of Triton and Gluon kernels, including quantization, embedding, normalization, fusions, GEMMs, Attention, and MoE, for [AITER](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter). He is also responsible for kernel integration, bridging AMD's high-performance software stack to a variety of LLM serving platforms, such as ATOM, vLLM and SGLang.
