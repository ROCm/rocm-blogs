# Charles Yang

Charles Yang is a Senior AI Software Architect in the AI Group at AMD. Charles specializes in AI performance full-stack optimization across CPU, GPU, and NPU platforms, LLM model quantization, and 3D neural rendering pipelines. With approximately 13 years of experience in the technology sector, he holds 7 granted U.S. patents and has published multiple papers at top-tier AI conferences including CVPR etc. in deep learning domain. He earned his MS degree from Beijing Institute of Technology.
