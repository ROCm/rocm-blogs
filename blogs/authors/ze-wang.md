# Ze Wang

Ze is a Research Scientist at the AMD GenAI Team. His research focuses on image and video generation. Before joining AMD, Ze worked as a Postdoctoral Rsearch Asscocate at the Electrical and Computer Engineering Department of Purdue University, where he earned his Ph.D. in 2023. During his Ph.D. study, Ze did research internships at Microsoft Azure AI, Facebook AI, and Volvo R&D.
