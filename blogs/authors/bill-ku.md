Bill Ku is a System Application Engineer on AMD’s Data Center GPU team, having joined AMD in March 2025. His work focuses on ROCm and container delivery pipelines, CI/CD automation, and the development of RDC exporters. With prior experience in cloud computing and SRE, he is actively involved in integrating Kubernetes with data center platforms, bridging GPU software stacks with production-scale infrastructure.
