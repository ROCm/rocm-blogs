Yi-Jun (Eveline) Chen is a Senior Software Development Engineer on AMD’s Data Center GPU team, having joined AMD in April 2024. Her work focuses on the performance analysis, tuning, optimization, and deployment of mainstream Large Language Models (LLMs). Prior to joining AMD, she worked at Intel, where she developed machine learning systems for Chromebook platforms. She also possesses extensive experience in computer vision, AI model architecture design, and deep learning research.
