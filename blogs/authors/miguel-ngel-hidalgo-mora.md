# Miguel Ángel Hidalgo Mora

Miguel Ángel Hidalgo Mora (JartX) is Lead AI Engineer at INCloud Solutions in Murcia, Spain, an official vLLM reviewer and an AMD Developer Ambassador. He authored Octave, the low-bit KV-cache quantization merged in vLLM, with HIP kernels for both RDNA3 (Radeon RX 7900 XTX) and CDNA3 (Instinct MI300X), and develops RDNA3 HIP kernels for W4A16 GEMMs and MoE that let large quantized models run fast on consumer Radeon GPUs.
