# Luka Tsabadze

Luka Tsabadze is a Sr. Software Development Engineer in the AI Group at AMD, specializing in scientific AI workloads. His work spans optimizing training, inference, and deployment of models on AMD GPUs. His previous experience includes the full lifecycle of large language models from pretraining and finetuning to production deployment, speech recognition both streaming and offline, edge AI deployments, and recommendation systems.
