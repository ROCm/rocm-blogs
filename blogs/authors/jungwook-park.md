# Jungwook Park

Jungwook Park is a Senior Member of Technical Staff at AMD, where he works on the Triton compiler backend for AMD GPUs. His work focuses on profiling and optimizing machine learning kernels using compiler-based techniques. He has a background in GPU architecture and OpenCL driver development. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Yonsei University.
