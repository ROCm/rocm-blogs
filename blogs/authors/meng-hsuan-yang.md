Meng-Hsuan Yang is a Senior Member of Technical Staff on AMD’s Data Center GPU team, focusing on AI operator kernel development and performance optimization. Meng-Hsuan joined AMD in 2025 after working at MediaTek, where he contributed to the AI compiler team with a focus on operator-level tuning, model performance optimization, and multi-core execution efficiency. His areas of interest include AI compiler optimization, GPU kernel tuning, and efficient deep learning inference.
