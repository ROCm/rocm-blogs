# Tanya Roosta

Tanya Roosta is Director of Program Management at AMD, where she leads research programs in AI algorithms and Physical AI. She has strong experience taking AI/ML from research into production at scale, including LLMs, federated learning, and secure ML, from her time leading applied science teams in Search and Alexa AI at Amazon. Tanya got her PhD from UC Berkeley and teaches ML in UC Berkeley’s graduate data science program.
