# Nusrat Islam

Nusrat Sharmin Islam is a Principal Member of Technical Staff (PMTS) software engineer at AMD, specializing in high-performance computing, AI communication libraries, and distributed systems. She earned her Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from The Ohio State University. Prior to joining AMD, Nusrat spent 6.5 years at Intel, contributing to the design and optimization of scalable system software and performance-critical technologies. At AMD, she has continued to advance software innovations for large-scale computing, AI, and communication frameworks. Nusrat’s research and engineering contributions span parallel programming, collective communication, high-performance interconnects, storage systems, and distributed computing. She has authored numerous widely cited publications in these areas and is passionate about bridging cutting-edge research with production-scale software systems.
