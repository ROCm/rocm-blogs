# Nima Poulad

Nima is a software Engineering Manager at AMD's AI division, where he leads a team that enables and optimizes AI training and inference workloads on AMD Instinct™ GPUs. He has over 16 years of software engineering experience in the industry and enjoys working side by side with partners and the open-source community to make AI run at its best on AMD hardware. Nima holds a B.Sc. in Mathematics with a specialization in Robotics Systems from Queen's University.
