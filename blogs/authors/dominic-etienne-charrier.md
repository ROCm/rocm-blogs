# Dominic Etienne Charrier

Dominic Etienne Charrier is an SMTS Software Development Engineer with AMD's AI Open-Source Software group (AIOSS). He obtained his PhD in Computer Science from Durham University (UK) and is trained in numerical methods and the optimization of their performance on heterogeneous hardware. Dominic joined AMD six years ago and contributed to some early AMD MI wins in the HPC space in Europe as well as to ROCm projects like the HIP Fortran interfaces (hipFORT).

Currently, Dominic works on AMD's ROCm Data Science (ROCm-DS) toolkit on projects such as hipDF and Numba HIP. The topic of programming agility, which involves deterministic and AI-assisted automatic code generation and simplifying and automating software ports, plays a big role in his work and vision.
