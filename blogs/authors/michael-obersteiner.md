# Michael Obersteiner

Michael is a Member of Technical Staff (MTS) open source software engineer in the AIOSS organization. He obtained his PhD in Computer Science from the Technical University of Munich, where he worked for 8 years as a PhD candidate and Post-Doc in Scientific Computing and HPC. He is especially interested in combining mathematical concepts and (parallel) algorithms to achieve high performance and accurate numerical calculations.

Michael spends his time at AMD contributing to and maintaining different projects in the open source ROCm-DS software stack for AMD Instinct™ GPUs.
