# Kelin Zeng

Kelin Zeng (Colin) is a Software Development Engineer in AMD’s AIG Quark team. He focuses on AI model quantization research, inference engine quantization integration and performance optimization, and quantization tooling development. He received his Bachelor of Science degree in Computer Engineering from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.
