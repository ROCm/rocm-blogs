Prakamya Mishra is an Applied Research Engineer in the GenAI team at AMD, where he is working on novel techniques to efficiently train foundation models. He did his master's in computer science from University of Massachusetts Amherst. Before joining AMD, he worked as a researcher at the BioNLP lab at UMass Amherst, Applied Scientist intern at Amazon Alexa SLU team, graduate researcher at Alexa NLU team, and Research Intern at IBM, where his work focused LLMs and their applications in generative AI.
