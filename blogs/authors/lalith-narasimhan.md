# Lalith Narasimhan

Lalith Narasimhan is a Software Engineering Manager at AMD, where he leads the development of Horizontal SDKs that enable AI and data science workloads within AMD's open-source software ecosystem for Instinct™ GPUs. He holds a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Western Michigan University, where his research focused on accelerating wireless communication and signal processing algorithms using GPUs. Lalith is passionate about advancing GPU-accelerated AI and empowering developers to innovate within AMD's open-source AI ecosystem.
