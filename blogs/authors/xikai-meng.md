# Xikai Meng

Xikai (Noah) Meng is a Software Engineer on the AMD Quark team, specializing in efficient AI inference for large-scale foundation models. His work focuses on quantization, speculative decoding, and cross-platform inference optimization, aiming to enhance the performance, efficiency, and deployment experience of modern deep learning systems. He received his Bachelor’s degree in Physics from Harbin Institute of Technology.
