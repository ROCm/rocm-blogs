# Mehmet Cagri Kaymak

Mehmet Cagri Kaymak is a software engineer at AMD, where he designs and tunes GPU kernels for large language model inference on Instinct accelerators. He develops Triton and Gluon kernels for AITER, AMD's ML operators library, and drives their integration into production serving platforms such as ATOM and vLLM. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Michigan State University.
